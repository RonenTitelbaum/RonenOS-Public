# Your OS, your choice

Use Ronen OS locally, keep your own private repository, or keep an existing
system. Contributions are optional. Leaving and returning are welcome.

## Donate only what you choose

1. Choose a pseudonym and a small reusable prompt, workflow, code improvement,
   redacted experiment, or bug report. Start from [the template](contributions/TEMPLATE.md).
2. Keep your profile, raw chat history, credentials, contacts, personal account
   records and unselected progress private. Inspect the exact proposed file.
3. Confirm that the selected content may be public. A public repository, fork,
   branch or draft pull request is not a private review channel. Public copies
   and Git history may remain after a deletion request.
4. With existing verified upstream write access, use your provisioned
   `donations/<pseudonym>` branch. Otherwise [fork the public repository](https://github.com/RonenTitelbaum/RonenOS-Public/fork),
   create that branch in your fork, add only your selected donation, and open a
   draft pull request targeting `donations/inbox` after reviewing the diff.
5. The maintainer may provision a personal upstream branch after review. It is
   pending until its actual URL is confirmed. No owner credentials or blanket
   repository write access are handed to contributors.

Suggested file: `contributions/<pseudonym>/<short-topic>.md`. Contribution data
never becomes runtime policy automatically. The public export allowlist stays
closed until a maintainer explicitly reviews and approves an addition; an
unreviewed extra file may therefore fail the public export check.

GitHub's [branch/fork guidance](https://docs.github.com/en/pull-requests/concepts/writing-code-for-a-project)
explains the permission distinction. Without GitHub tools or an account, prepare
the contribution as a local text file. Preparing is not sending.

## Returning after independent development

The return offer is **4.2069% off** an eligible resubscription for someone who
tried developing their own system and permits review of selected progress.
Progress may be unfinished or unsuccessful. There is no minimum donation size,
success requirement, or requirement to publish it publicly.

Choose the exact summary or diff and identify the recipient, channel and
visibility before sending. A private conversation with your own GPT is not
proof that Ronen received your progress. If no verified private channel exists,
keep the review packet local until one is agreed; public upload is optional.
Permission to read progress and permission to publish it are separate choices.

The [machine-readable policy](policies/return_discount.json) records the exact
rate. Pricing, eligible plans, duration, stacking and billing are not configured
in this repository. No payment or discount application occurs from loading the
TXT. Confirm the plan, terms and final quote before any real resubscription.
